▸ Methodology & assumptions

This estimate combines a records-based model with fixed breach costs and an AI-specific risk premium. It is a directional estimate for discussion, not a guarantee.

Baselines (IBM Cost of a Data Breach 2024): global average breach $4.88M; U.S. average $9.36M; per-record cost varies by data type (~$165 general PII to ~$408 health); average 267 days to identify and contain.

Per-record cost by sensitivity: non-sensitive $60, PII $165, financial $250, defense/CUI $300, PHI $408.

Detection & Response = 0.3% of revenue (floor $250K, cap $3M). Lost Business & Downtime = records cost + 0.5% of revenue (floor $200K). Regulatory = conservative expected exposure per selected regime (GDPR ~0.3% revenue, HIPAA ~$500K, PCI ~$200K, state privacy modeled per litigated record, CMMC ~$1M contract-eligibility loss) — well below statutory maximums (GDPR alone can reach 4% of global revenue).

AI Risk Premium multiplies the subtotal by exposure level: none 1.0×, internal 1.15×, customer-facing 1.35×, autonomous agents 1.6× — reflecting expanded attack surface (prompt injection, agent data leakage, tool-abuse chains) that traditional breach models omit. Range shown is 0.6× (contained) to 1.8× (severe/mega-breach).

Not legal or financial advice. Figures are modeled estimates for scoping conversations. Your actual exposure depends on controls, response maturity, and incident specifics — which is exactly what an audit measures.
