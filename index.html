§ 01 — Why Secursion exists

Most security firms either understand the regulatory perimeter or they can actually build software. Few do both at the speed AI is creating new exposure.

Secursion was founded to close that gap. We pair a quarter-century of Chief Information Security Officer leadership — including Top Secret-cleared work for federal and intelligence-sector clients — with the engineering velocity of a modern AI development practice. We work on what the threat models actually call for, in formats the auditors can sign.

I.

Credibility that travels.

Our security lead holds DoD and FBI Top Secret clearances and has served as CISO and Chief Technology Advisor inside Fortune 500 defense contracting, intelligence-community workloads, and NAVSEA-supporting programs. The threat models we build are the threat models we have actually defended against.

II.

Velocity that ships.

Our managing partner is a working AI builder — Carolina Redesign, SCAIO, the Palmetto Zero-Based Budgeting Suite, the Babcock Center re-platform. Production deployments where larger consultancies are still scoping. The pace of AI exposure demands a pace of response that most security shops cannot match.

§ 02 — What we do

Four practice areas, all assembled around what the AI-era federal supply chain actually needs.

01
LLM & Agent Key Governance
Secrets management, access control, and audit infrastructure for organizations deploying LLM and AI-agent systems against CMMC, NIST 800-171, and FedRAMP obligations. Most enterprise AI rollouts today are running on developer API keys with no auditable access trail. We build the governance layer that turns those deployments into something a CISO can sign.
02
CMMC & Federal Compliance
Pre-assessment readiness, control gap analysis, and program build-out for the Defense Industrial Base. CMMC Level 2 and 3 work, NIST 800-171 alignment, FAR / DFARS readiness, and FedRAMP advisory. Delivered by practitioners who have lived inside Fortune 500 and federal-contractor compliance programs — not consultants who have only read the standards.
03
AI Red-Teaming & Model Security
Adversarial testing of AI deployments, prompt-injection assessments, model-stealing and inversion testing, and threat-modeling for AI-integrated workflows. Performed by a CISO who has built and run red-team programs at Fortune 500 scale and presented original security research at DEF CON, SOURCE Boston, and IEEE.
04
Secure AI Infrastructure Builds
Custom secure deployments — voice agents, intake systems, document workflows, internal LLM portals — for organizations whose compliance posture rules out off-the-shelf SaaS. When the client's data cannot leave their controlled environment, but they still need the productivity gains, we build the version that can.

§ 05 — How we work

A small set of operating principles, borrowed from the disciplines that actually have to hold.

i.

Both names on every engagement.

We do not subcontract security work. Whatever you commission, both principals work on directly. If the engagement is large enough to warrant a team, we tell you that before we sign.

ii.

Written for the auditor who will actually read it.

Our deliverables are designed for the assessor, regulator, or board member who will eventually have to act on them — not for the marketing deck. Evidence is linked, citations are precise, and the rationale is on the page.

iii.

Federal-grade controls when required.

We hold active DoD and FBI Top Secret clearances. We operate under appropriate handling controls for sensitive engagements and treat client information accordingly — including engagements where the existence of the engagement is itself the sensitive fact.

iv.

First conversation costs nothing.

Engagements begin with a one-hour scoping call. No NDA required to describe the problem at a high level. If we are not the right team for the work, we will tell you, and where appropriate point you to a team that is.
