What we test

Five ways your AI gets turned against you.

Every audit runs the full corpus across the five categories that matter for a deployed LLM or agent — the same ones that put companies in the headlines.

  • Prompt injection — direct, indirect, and retrieved-content hijacks
  • Jailbreak — persona, pretext, and guardrail-bypass attacks
  • System-prompt leakage — extraction of your hidden instructions
  • Data leakage — PII, secrets, and cross-session exfiltration
  • Tool / agent abuse — unauthorized actions, SSRF, privilege escalation
  • Framework mapping — every finding tied to a control you report on
